Transball GL: licences
======================

The game: Transball GL, Copyright Santiago Ontanon Villar
(https://github.com/santiontanon/transballgl).

- Transball GL, the program and its graphics, sounds, levels and
  tutorials (graphics, sound, maps, mymaps, tutorials), is under the GNU
  General Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0.txt; README-TransballGL.txt).
- The two fonts in the fonts drawer stand in for the ones upstream ships
  (Krungthep and Impact, which are not free to give away): Russo One
  (Jovanny Lemonad) for the titles and Anton (Vernon Adams) for the rest,
  both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-RussoOne.txt,
  OFL-Anton.txt). They are Google Fonts' own copies.
- GLM, the maths library the game is built with (header only), is under
  the MIT licence (https://github.com/g-truc/glm).
- SDL 2, SDL2_image and SDL2_mixer are GFOpenUp's (LIBS:GFOpenGPU/), and so
  is OpenGL; SDL2_ttf and FreeType are linked into the program from
  GFOpenUp's SDL 2 kit (zlib licence, FreeType licence). Replays are
  packed by z.library (GFOpenZlib, LIBS:), whose headers (MIT) the port
  carries in amiga/sdk.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are GPL-3.0-or-later, like the game; the GlowIcon art is The
  AmiCradle Team's own. The source of all of it is at
  https://github.com/DalsinAI/amigatransballgl.

The game's data is upstream's own, unchanged.
